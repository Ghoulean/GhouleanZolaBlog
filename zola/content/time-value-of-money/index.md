Baldur's Gate 3 is a role-playing game where the player and their companions starts at level 1 and gains experience primarily through completing quests, discovering locations, and winning fights. The maximum level the player and their companions can reach is level 12, at which point gaining experience does nothing.

It's rather easy to reach level 12 before reaching the final boss, so one may conclude that trying to "grind" experience is unnecessary. Why put in that kind of work if you'll end up level 12 anyways?

But grinding *can* pay off *if* you put the early levels to work. If you're someone like me, more power = more fun. By leveling faster, you reach your level 12 build faster and get to play with your toys for longer.

# Time Preference

[**Time preference**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_preference) is the idea that spending money to receive a good or service earlier may be more valuable than doing so later. This is similar to [Time Value of Money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_value_of_money) (TVM), but TVM is geared towards *receiving* money whereas time preference is about *spending* it.

In the Baldur's Gate 3 example, I decided to spend time grinding *earlier* in the game to get stronger and thus have more fun.
