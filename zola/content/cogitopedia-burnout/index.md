I can't do this. The amount of work required to "fix" Cogitopedia is too high.

## The bad, and the ugly

The Lobocorp section and Ruina section are incredibly barebones. They need a complete overhaul in order to enter a usable state. And I've bitten off way more than I can chew with this project.

Cogitopedia is objectively not a well-run project. The wiki has been in-development for over two years and only the Distortion Detective section is good. The number of contributors is extremely low (roughly 10) -- there's a huge issue in advertising, recruiting, and maintaining manpower. The wiki is basically three subwikis in a trenchcoat, plus a little extra for Distortion Detective and Wonderlab. As far as I can tell, only DD is complete.

But the leadership is absent. I don't blame them -- they've been solely responsible for the CSS of the site (a major headache, I know) and they've set up a lot of the currently existing templates that the site uses. But these templates and CSS also suffer from cruft. The leadership seemed...*thrust* into the position moreso than volunteered. The inexperience and "learning on the job" is evident. Not a bad thing, to be clear. Everyone has to start somewhere. But the thought of going back and *redoing everything that's been done already* is so daunting and intimidating that burnout is inevitable.

Knowledge is also incredibly silo'd. Out of the very few contributors, like 3 people appeared comfortable with templating. Admin, one other person, and myself. Two people are comfortable with handling the CSS. Admin, and myself. I joined the project for only ~a month. Again, the technical leadership is lacking, and this is faultless. The lack of diversity among the staff (exacerbated due to low manpower) means that essential skills needed to maintain the wiki aren't being properly educated among the regular contributors. Those who know are too busy figuring out how to do the work that needs doing rather than sharing their skills to others.

A lot of the content can be automated, in my eyes. Transcripting the Limbus story logs, setting up the Lobocorp abno pages (and I have the generated wikitexts on local...just need to mass-update), auto-generating the Ruina game data pages... But again, automating all of this requires a technical background that at most two people possess.

I need to find someone more dedicated and able to take the project the rest of the way. Unfortunately, said person:

1. Must be sufficiently motivated to re-document everything from the Fandom to the Cogitopedia
2. Has the leadership ability to organize the wiki contents while respecting non-Lobocorp content
3. Has the technical ability to set up page content such as formatting, templating, UI/UX design, in addition to utilizing automation when needed
4. Has the domain knowledge to populate articles with detailed and accurate content, both from a technical perspective (gameplay logic, stats, etc) and from a lore perspective
5. Motivated to do all of this in their free time
6. Has said free time to spend

But the number of people who match all of this is zero. Well, one. Me currently. Except I don't fit #1. Nor #4. And my #5 is rapidly dwindling. And my #6 is going to evaporate soon (I'm currently interviewing for a new position in my career). So it's zero.

I've checked #lobotomy-lounge in PMCH but the clientelle there is disheartening. Although they regularly bash on Fandom, and constantly lament for a better alternative, I've had very little success in inspiring those into contributing to Cogitopedia and sticking to it.

I'll automate the Lobocorp abnos at the very minimum. But beyond that, for the near- and immediate future, this project is shelved.
